Partner Proposal

Turn the story into a system: a site that books demos, and a back end that never lets a lead go cold.

You’ve built something genuinely ahead of the market: Ireland’s first 360° automated spray tan booth, a credible regulatory tailwind, and a clear B2B plan to convert salons, gyms and UV franchises. The investor story is sharp. What’s missing is the digital engine that turns interest into booked demos, and the CRM back-end that captures, nurtures and converts every enquiry rather than letting it sit in an inbox. That’s where we start.

Where we start

Two gaps between the pitch and the pipeline.

The deck and the strategy are ready to win the room. But the two things that actually generate and hold demand aren’t built yet. Both are foundational, and both are the right place to begin.

01 · THE WEBSITE

A site that demos, not just describes.

The current site is a polished consumer concept: “find a booth, book a session.” But your Year 1 goal is B2B: get salon owners, gym managers and franchise operators to book a demo at Omeath. That needs a different site with a single job.

Right now there are no working forms, no lead capture, no ROI calculator logic, no tracking, and no routing of enquiries anywhere. It looks the part but can’t convert.

02 · THE BACK-END

A CRM that catches every lead.

A demo booking is only valuable if it’s followed up fast and never forgotten. There’s currently no CRM, no lead pipeline, no automated nurture, and no email sequencing behind the site.

We’ll build the lead-management and automation back end, with AI where it earns its place, so every enquiry is captured, scored, sequenced and moved toward a booked demo without manual chasing.

The rollout

Web first. CRM second. Then we scale it.

We start where the leverage is: the conversion site and the system behind it. Once the engine captures and converts, we layer on the channels that feed it. Strategy before spend, foundations before amplification.
