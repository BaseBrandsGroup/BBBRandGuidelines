01

Our Approach

From our initial conversation

We Are Perform revolves around a physiotherapy practice treating pain through to performance, with a view to expanding via online, evergreen programmes in the near future. Here’s the brief the audit is being designed against, based on what we took away from our conversation.

“The current site feels outdated and is holding back our SEO. There’s some confusion around brand messaging and niche, and time is tight — we’re balancing in-person clients with the need to actually work on the business.”

  • Brand confusion. Overall messaging and niche need sharpening — clarity on who We Are Perform is for and what separates pain treatment from performance work.
  • Time pressure. Balancing in-person clients with the need to grow the business means marketing decisions need to be high-confidence and low-effort to act on.
  • Outdated website. The current site is impacting SEO performance and inhibiting any marketing effort that follows. It needs auditing before any spend is committed.
  • Strategy before spend. The audit and strategic report comes first so that future activity is grounded in data, insight, and clear direction — not assumptions.
  • Local Google footprint. Improving visibility in the local area is the priority, with paid Google Ads layered in once the foundations are right.
  • Evergreen programmes ahead. The site needs to be ready to support online, evergreen programme expansion further down the line, not just in-person bookings.
